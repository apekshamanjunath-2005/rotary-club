Who We Are

Over Four Decades of Dedicated Fellowship & Impact

Chartered in 1982 under Rotary International District 3170, the Rotary Club of Honavar brings together doctors, engineers, educators, entrepreneurs, and civic leaders. We channel our professional vocations toward sustainable healthcare, green ecology, coastal literacy, and community empowerment.

Guiding Ethical Compass

The Rotary 4-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do:

1

Is it the TRUTH?

Uncompromising integrity and transparency in all civic and personal interactions.

2

Is it FAIR to all concerned?

Promoting equitable access, mutual respect, and social justice for all community members.

3

Will it build GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS?

Fostering deep camaraderie, regional harmony, and international fellowship.

4

Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?

Ensuring enduring, positive value and sustainable outcomes for generations to come.
